VITREA

The glass frontier

Vitrea was meant to be a quiet mining colony. Then the storms came — walls of shattered glass that scour the plains and leave living crystal in their wake.

The colony company is long gone. Its claims are not. Rival outfits now fight over every glowing vein, and the crystal keeps growing.

Build your base. Harvest the crystal. Break your rival before the next storm breaks you.

How to play
  • Your harvester gathers crystal automatically; the refinery turns it into credits.
  • Build from the sidebar: power first, then factories. Place buildings on rock near your base.
  • Drag to select units, right-click to move or attack, Ctrl+right-click to attack-move.
  • Double-click a unit to select all of that type on screen.
  • Shift+1–9 saves a group, 1–9 recalls it. X stops. P pauses. + / − change the game speed.
  • Shift+right-click queues waypoints. E selects your whole army, H jumps home, Space jumps to the last attack.
  • Select a factory and right-click the ground to set a rally point for its new units.
  • Sidebar: Shift-click queues five, Ctrl-click keeps a unit in production. Letters on the buttons are hotkeys (Shift for units).
  • Z sells and C repairs buildings. Hold Alt (or press `) to see every health bar. Shift+F1–F4 bookmarks the camera; F1–F4 jumps back.
  • Build a Radar Outpost to switch on your minimap.
  • A Tech Center unlocks your heavy unit and your hero. Select the hero and press Q for its ability.
  • Units that deal enough damage rank up: Veteran, Elite, Heroic.
  • Get out of the way of crystal storms — they shred infantry.
  • Destroy every enemy building to win.

Play online

1v1 against a friend. The host's map, storms and fog settings are used; each player brings the faction picked on the right.
